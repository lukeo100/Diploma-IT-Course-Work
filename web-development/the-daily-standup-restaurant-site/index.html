Our Story

From a kitchen in a shared flat to your favourite local.

It started with a hackathon in 2017. Two software developers — Sam and Jordan — were pulling an all-nighter at a startup weekend when they hit a wall. Not a coding wall, but a hunger wall. The vending machine was empty, the pizza place had closed, and the office microwave had mysteriously stopped working.

At 3 AM, Sam pulled out a portable stove and a bag of ingredients they'd packed "just in case." Jordan raided the pantry for spices. Within thirty minutes, the most incredible aromas filled the co-working space. Other hackers abandoned their keyboards and gathered around. That night, over a makeshift meal of spicy noodles and garlic bread, an idea was born.

🍳 The legendary 3 AM noodles

"We spent our days writing code and our nights dreaming about food," Jordan recalled in a 2019 interview with Bite Magazine. "One day we just looked at each other and said: what if we did the thing we actually loved?"

Sam had spent years working as a chef before pivoting to software engineering during the tech boom. Jordan had always been the one hosting dinner parties, experimenting with fusion recipes, and running food blogs on the side. Together, they had the perfect blend of technical precision and culinary creativity.

Milestones

2017

The Hackathon Meal

Sam and Jordan's legendary 3 AM meal sparks an idea at a Melbourne startup weekend.

2018

The First Pop-Up

Operating out of a shared commercial kitchen, they run weekend pop-ups from a converted food truck. Word spreads fast.

2019

Doors Open

The Daily Standup opens its permanent home in Fitzroy, Melbourne. The name is a nod to their agile roots — a place to gather, share, and fuel up.

2021

The Expansion

After weathering the toughest years in hospitality, we expand to include a dedicated coffee bar and co-working nook.

Today

Still Shipping

Continuously deploying great food and coffee. No regressions, only features.

"Every great standup starts with good fuel. We just happen to serve ours on a plate."

— Sam & Jordan, Co-Founders
