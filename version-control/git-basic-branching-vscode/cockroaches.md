# Cockroach Facts

- Cockroaches are insects that have lived on Earth for millions of years.
- They are mostly active at night.
- Cockroaches can eat many kinds of food, including crumbs and paper.
- They can survive for about a week without their head.
- Cockroaches can run very fast for their size.
- Some species can live for a month without food.
- They prefer warm, dark, and damp places.
